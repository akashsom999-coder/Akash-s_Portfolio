About Me

I'm an MCA student at Lovely Professional University, with a Bachelor of Computer Applications from Maa Shakumbhari University behind me. Somewhere between coursework and side projects, I found that I genuinely enjoy the process of turning a real-world problem into working software.

My focus sits at the intersection of software development and web development — I like tracing a problem down to its structure, then building something people can actually use. Whether that's a matching algorithm, a role-based dashboard, or a clean REST API, I care about the solution being practical, not just technically correct.

I'm comfortable moving across the stack — Python and Django on the backend, JavaScript and React on the front, SQL underneath it all — and I treat every project as a chance to pick up something new. Right now, that curiosity is pointed squarely at building user-focused software that solves problems worth solving.
